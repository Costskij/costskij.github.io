About Me

I am an Italian photographer whose journey into visual storytelling began with a background in Law. My passion for artistic expression led me to deepen my creative pathway through drawing, painting, and ultimately photography.

My practice evolved internationally as an artist in residence painting in Lisbon, and later in Athens, working on set as an assistant for editorial photography productions.

Since then, I have contributed to high-end campaigns, editorial shoots—including collaborations with Vogue Hong Kong in Morocco—and commercial productions across Saudi Arabia, mainland Greece, and luxury resorts around the globe, working alongside top industry professionals.
